Our story

Parenting advice that's actually been lived, not just studied.

Paranneting started as one parent's answer to a question every parent asks at 2am: is anyone else figuring this out as they go too?

Why Paranneting exists

Most parenting advice is either too clinical to use in the middle of a tantrum, or too vague to trust. Paranneting was built to close that gap: real strategies, explained plainly, from someone who has actually tried them.

What began as sharing honest, in-the-trenches parenting moments grew into a full support system, coaching sessions, practical resources, and a community of parents who'd rather trade real advice than perform perfect parenting.

What we believe

There's no single "right way"

What works depends on your kid, your household, and your values. We help you find your version of it.

Small changes compound

You don't need to overhaul your parenting. You need the next right step, and someone to help you find it.

Honesty over performance

We talk about the hard days, not just the highlight reel. That's where the useful advice actually lives.

Parents deserve support too

You can't pour from an empty cup. Part of raising grounded kids is taking care of the person raising them.
